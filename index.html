What I look for in a game

The things I keep coming back to, and the games that do them best.

Self identity

A game that is unapologetically itself. Games like: Outerwilds, GTFO, Titanfall, Armored core 6, Frostpunk.

Meaningful Mastery

Players grow through skillful play, not artificial progression. Games like: Elden ring, Black myth Wukong.

Elegant Complexity

Depth emerges from the interaction of simple, well-defined mechanics. Games like: Satisfactory, Balatro, Doom.

My all-time favourites

  • Titanfall 2

    Fast and constant fun in 20 minute bursts. Just long enough.

  • Doom Eternal

    Fast, bloody and engaging. What more could you want?

  • GTFO

    Brutal but rewarding the multiplayer update.

  • Outerwilds

    The best game you can never play again.
